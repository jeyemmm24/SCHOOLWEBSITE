Admissions

We welcome future leaders, innovators, and global citizens. Begin your journey at AralMuna by following our simple admission steps.

Step 1: Online Application

Fill out the application form with accurate student details. No need to visit the campus.

Step 2: Document Submission

Upload required documents such as birth certificate, report card, and recommendation letters.

Step 3: Entrance Assessment

Applicants take an online or onsite entrance assessment to evaluate their readiness.

Step 4: Interview

Selected students and parents will be invited for an interview with our faculty.

Step 5: Enrollment

Once accepted, you will receive an admission offer and payment instructions for enrollment.
